Packet loss is the percentage of unanswered ICMP probes, measured separately for public targets and the local router. Single-target misses and local-path failures are retained as noise because they do not prove an ISP outage. A confirmed upstream outage requires the Wi-Fi link and router to remain reachable while all three ICMP probes and two independent TCP/443 confirmations fail. If the router also fails, this PC alone cannot distinguish the laptop, Wi-Fi access point, or router; use the type filter to review that evidence separately.
